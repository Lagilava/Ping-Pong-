Ping Pong - Neon Edition is fast, glowing arcade table tennis built for your
thumbs.

SIX WAYS TO PLAY
- Classic: pure, fast pong against an AI that feints, aims and learns your habits.
- Zombie: hold the line against waves of zombies while you rally.
- Gravity: bend the ball around planets and wells, and open a wormhole.
- Speed: every hit gets faster. Overcharge when you dare.
- Obstacle: bounce through a living course and roll the Lucky Dice.
- Custom: build your own arena, rules, paddles, ball, background and music in the Forge.

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Touch controls with an ability button for each mode
- Local two-player on one device
- Online matches with a friend using a 5-letter room code, no account needed
- Instant replays of the best rallies, achievements and levels
- Plays offline after the first launch

No ads. No accounts. No tracking.
