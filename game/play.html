Obstacle Mode

Run the course

  • Score past your opponent as usual. Hazards bounce the ball, and they speed up as the rally grows.
  • Hit every glowing checkpoint in order in one run for +1 point. If the other side touches the ball, the run breaks.
  • Coins arm a shield that blocks one goal against you. ? blocks give coins, a Turbo shot or Star power. Against the computer, only your shots collect them.
  • Boost pads launch the ball. The course layout changes every 4 points.
  • Tap Space to roll the Lucky Dice.Tap the Lucky Dice button with your left thumb to roll. 6 Star power, 5 shooting balls, 4 pull the ball to your paddle, 3 big paddle, 2 no luck, 1 fast ball. Use Enter for player 2.
Zombie Mode

Hold the line

  • Clear each wave by hitting every zombie, or score past the guardian until its health is empty.
  • 5 lives for the whole run. A missed ball or a zombie reaching your barricade costs one.
  • Hold Space to pull zombiesHold the Pull button with your left thumb to drag zombies into your barricade for points. No life lost. Pull reaches only nearby zombies above and below your paddle, and uses energy (the right mouse button works too).
  • Pick a reward after every wave. Hit glowing orbs for extra powers.
  • Wave 10 is the boss fight. Knock the boss out to win.
